Mastering Adverbs of Manner

An adverb of manner is a word that describes how an action is done. Most of them end in -ly!

👀 4 Ways to Write the Same Sentence!

Watch how we can shift the adverb slowly around to change the rhythm of our sentence without changing its basic meaning:

🎬 1. Front

Slowly, the giant turtle crawled across the hot sand.

(Requires a capital letter and a comma!)
👉 2. Pre-Verb

The giant turtle slowly crawled across the hot sand.

(Placed directly before the action word!)
👈 3. Post-Verb

The giant turtle crawled slowly across the hot sand.

(Placed directly after the action verb!)
🏁 4. End

The giant turtle crawled across the hot sand slowly.

(Placed at the very end of the sentence!)
