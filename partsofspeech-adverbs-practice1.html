What is an Adverb of Manner?

An adverb of manner describes how an action is done. It tells us more about the verb! Most of these adverbs end in -ly.

Example:

The dog loudly barked.

"Loudly" is the adverb because it describes how the dog "barked" (the verb). Notice that adverbs can jump around in sentences!
