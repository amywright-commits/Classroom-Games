Shades of Meaning

An Adjective Intensity Gradient Activity

📋

How to Play:

  • You will see a "paint swatch" card with 4 color slots.
  • Sort the adjectives based on their intensity.
  • Place the least intense adjective at the top (lightest color).
  • Place the most intense adjective at the bottom (darkest color).
  • Click a word to move it, or drag and drop it into a slot!
  • Watch out! The words get harder as you progress.
