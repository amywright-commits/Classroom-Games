End Punctuation / 4 Types of Sentences

Before we practice our Lesson 2 vocabulary, review the chart below to remember how words change based on their sentence's purpose!

Purpose Type End Punctuation Example
Statement: You are stating or declaring something. declarative . The heart of a shrimp is located in its head.
Exclamation: You are stating or exclaiming with emotion. exclamatory ! That birthday party was so much fun!
Question: You are wondering or interrogating. interrogative ? Why do I always forget where I put my car?
Demand: You are demanding something, and it's imperative it be done! It starts with a verb. imperative ! or . Come fold your laundry.
