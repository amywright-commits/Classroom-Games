Shades of Action

A Verb Intensity Gradient Activity

How to Play:

  • You will see a "paint swatch" card with 4 color slots.
  • Sort the action verbs based on their intensity.
  • Place the least intense action at the top (lightest color).
  • Place the most intense action at the bottom (darkest color).
  • Click a word to move it, or drag and drop it into a slot!
